Baby Category: Inverted Root Cause Analysis

Observation: The Baby category shows an artificially high 30-day "Abandonment Rate" (represented by the tall bar above).

Analysis: Unlike electronics or groceries, baby products (like bulk diapers) have a long replacement cycle. A user not purchasing for 30 days is satisfied and dormant, not burned. The Trust Badges successfully increased Trial Conversion, and we must extend the CCAR lookback window for Baby specifically to 60+ days to avoid false-positive abandonment alerts.
